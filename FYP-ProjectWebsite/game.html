FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EverMotion free?

Yes. This prototype was developed for academic purposes and can be downloaded free of charge.

What hardware is required?

A Windows PC with a webcam capable of running MediaPipe hand tracking.

Does EverMotion replace physiotherapy?

No. EverMotion is designed as a complementary rehabilitation tool to increase motivation.

Can therapists monitor progress?

Future versions aim to include therapist dashboards and detailed analytics.
