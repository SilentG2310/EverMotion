Story

A Journey Beyond Recovery

In the year 2148, rehabilitation has evolved beyond hospital walls. Patients no longer endure repetitive exercises—they enter EverMotion, an immersive virtual world where every movement shapes reality.

Once powered by the mysterious Lumina Core, EverMotion was created as a sanctuary where hope and technology worked together to restore both body and spirit. But when the Core shattered, its energy scattered across the virtual world, leaving entire districts without light.

Guided by two intelligent rehabilitation robots, players restore each fragment through movement-based challenges. Every successful therapy session rebuilds both the world around them and their own confidence.

Every movement heals two worlds, the one around you and the one within you.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EverMotion free?

Yes. This prototype was developed for academic purposes and can be downloaded free of charge.

What hardware is required?

A Windows PC with a webcam capable of running MediaPipe hand tracking.

Does EverMotion replace physiotherapy?

No. EverMotion is designed as a complementary rehabilitation tool to increase motivation.

Can therapists monitor progress?

Future versions aim to include therapist dashboards and detailed analytics.
