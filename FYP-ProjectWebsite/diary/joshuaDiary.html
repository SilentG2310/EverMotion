Week 18

9 August 2026 - 15 August 2026

Final Testing & User Testing

Work Done

  • Completed the development of the game in Unity.
  • Finalised and polished the 12 mini-games and their features.
  • Conducted final testing and fixed the remaining bugs and issues.
  • Completed the required project documentation and finalised the remaining documents.
  • Visited an old folks' home to meet with the end users and conduct user testing of the game.
  • Observed the end users interacting with the game and collected feedback on their experience.
  • Made final improvements to the game based on the feedback received from the end users.
  • Completed the final version of the game and project documentation.

Challenges

  • Some minor bugs and issues were identified during the final testing and user testing sessions. These required additional adjustments before the project could be completed.
  • Some feedback from the end users required us to make small changes to improve the usability and overall experience of the game.

Reflection

  • Week 18 marked the final week of our project and was focused on completing both the game and the required documentation. One of the most important parts of this week was visiting an old folks' home to meet with our intended end users and allow them to test the game. This gave us an opportunity to observe how the users interacted with the mini-games and understand whether the game was easy and enjoyable for them to use.
  • The user testing session was especially valuable because it allowed us to receive feedback directly from the people who would be using the game. We were able to identify some minor usability issues and make final adjustments based on their feedback. After completing the testing and making the necessary improvements, we finalised the game and completed the remaining project documentation.
  • Overall, completing this project has given me valuable experience in Unity development, UI design, user testing, documentation, teamwork, and problem-solving. It was also rewarding to see the final product being used by the intended end users. Looking back at the project, I am satisfied with the progress we made from the initial planning stage to the completion of the final game.
