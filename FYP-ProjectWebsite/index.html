Games

Twelve Games, Twelve Challenges

Each game targets a specific movement — repair the robot’s systems one challenge at a time

01
Upper Limb · Reach

Sky Reach Protocol

“Restore Upper Power Grid”

Guide your robot toward a floating energy node with one hand, then raise and bend your other arm to grab it. Carry the node into the glowing zone above and lower your arm to install it — rushed grabs let it slip, steady carries get it home.

01 / 12
02
Upper Limb · Side Stretch

Lateral Circuit Recovery

“Restore the Circuits, Deflect Incoming Debris”

Debris and coolant capsules fall toward the circuit cables — swing your arms like blades to slice the debris before it connects, but let the coolant capsules through to boost your charge. Slice a coolant capsule by mistake and your blade short-circuits for a few seconds

02 / 12
03
Upper Limb · Steady Hold

Energy Flow Stabilisation

“Stabilise Power Flow”

Fly your robot through gaps in scrolling obstacle plating and grab glowing energy cells to keep the power flowing. Raise both arms out to your sides (T-pose) to climb, lower them to descend — weave through each gap as it shifts, and rest between waves to recover.

03 / 12
04
Upper Limb · Extension

Motion Drive Activation

“ Activate Motion Drive”

A goal zone lights up on a random side of the field — raise and extend your arm in that direction to push the robot and drive the block toward it. Reach the goal and a new one appears on another side, keeping you moving.

04 / 12
05
Upper Limb · Block

Pulse Defense Matrix

“Defend Control System”

Lasers strike from the left, right, or above — raise the matching arm to snap up a shield on that side, or raise both arms together to shield the top. Lower your arms once the laser passes to reset for the next strike

05 / 12
06
Elbow · Angle Control

Beam Alignment Sequence

“Align Energy Channels”

A colour-coded beam fires from your robot — raise your left or right elbow to rotate it toward the node that matches its colour. Land on the wrong colour and nothing happens, so keep rotating until you find the match; each hit repositions the node for the next round.

06 / 12
07
Wrist · Rotation

Rotation Core Calibration

“Calibrate Rotation Unit”

Point your fingertip to spin the inner ring toward the powered connector node — later stages add your other hand, then both together. Hold your wrist steady the instant it aligns to lock in the charge, then rotate on to the next connector as it lights up.

07 / 12
08
Wrist · Stability

Core Stability Protocol

“Stabilise Internal Energy”

Hold both hands out like a balance beam to tilt the platform and guide the sphere to its centre. Keep your hands steady while it glows and charges — and when a lightning warning flashes, a disturbance is coming, so rebalance fast before it rolls away.

08 / 12
09
Wrist · Point & Shoot

Pulse Release System

“Release Power Pulses”

Point your aim hand at a node appearing from one of eight directions, then fire with your other hand's gun-sign to destroy it. Cross your hands in front of the camera any time to swap which hand aims and which fires.

09 / 12
10
Finger · Precision Pinch

Micro Node Activation

“Activate Micro Nodes”

Glowing micro nodes flicker into view at random spots — pinch your thumb and index together and move your hand over one to collect it before it fades. Either hand works, so reach with whichever is closer.

10 / 12
11
Hand · Rhythm & Timing

Signal Sync Response

“Synchronise Systems”

Signal circles slide toward the hit zone, each colour-coded to the hand it needs — pinch with the matching hand (or both, for yellow) right as it arrives. Perfect timing scores highest, a miss breaks your combo, so stack consecutive hits to grow your multiplier.

11 / 12
12
Hand · Full Calibration

Energy Calibration Matrix

“Final Calibration — Engage Enemy Aircraft”

Point two fingers up, down, left, or right to dodge enemy aircraft dropping in columns and rockets streaking across the screen, and throw a rock sign to fire a laser straight up from your column. Rockets can't be shot down — only dodged — and getting hit by either stuns you, leaving you unable to move or fire until it wears off.

About

The Problem We're Solving

⚠

The Problem

Traditional rehabilitation exercises are repetitive and unengaging, causing users — especially elderly individuals and patients with motor impairments — to lose motivation and participate inconsistently.

◆

Our Solution

EverMotion allows users to perform rehabilitation exercises through natural hand movements using a standard webcam. By combining AI hand tracking with game mechanics, therapy becomes an engaging experience.

Why It Matters

📈 Improves motivation in rehabilitation
👤 Encourages consistent participation
💻 No specialised equipment needed
🧠 Accessible for elderly & motor-impaired users

Features

What EverMotion Offers

📷

Real-Time Hand Tracking

Uses MediaPipe via standard webcam — no gloves, controllers, or extra hardware needed.

👤

User Profiles & Avatars

Personalised accounts with avatar selection so each user has their own experience.

🎯

Hand Calibration

A short calibration flow adapts the game to each user's movement range and interaction ability.

📈

Adaptive Difficulty

Game elements like speed and positioning adjust automatically to suit the player's ability.

Technology

Under the Hood

Game Engine
Unity

Handles gameplay logic, UI, animations, and real-time rendering.

AI / Hand Tracking
MediaPipe

Real-time hand landmark detection from webcam input — no extra hardware required.

Game Programming
C#

Handles hand-tracking logic, player movement, input handling, and core game logic inside Unity.

2D UI & Sprites
Photoshop, Aseprite & Illustrator

Creation of game sprites, backgrounds, icons, and interface elements.

Project Management
Taiga & GitHub

Scrum sprint tracking via Taiga; continuous integration via GitHub.

Database
JSON

Stores user profiles, calibration data, and session statistics.
